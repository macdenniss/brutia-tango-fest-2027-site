Through the registration form we collect first name, last name, date of birth, email, telephone number, city, dance role, gender, registration type, selected package and, if provided, Facebook profile, dance partner and room-request details. We do not claim a specific international-transfer safeguard without verification of the terms applicable to the account in use. Data are accessible to the organising team and necessary technical providers. We do not sell data or share them for third-party advertising.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to processing by writing to info@pepertango.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.
