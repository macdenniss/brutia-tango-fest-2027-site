Controller and contacts

The data controller is Peper Bruzia ASD (Italian tax code 98138770783). For requests concerning personal data: info@pepertango.com.

Data collected and purpose

Through the registration form we collect first name, last name, date of birth, email, telephone number, city, dance role, gender, registration type, selected package and, if provided, Facebook profile, dance partner and room-request details. We use these data solely to manage registration for Brutia Tango Fest 2027, check availability and contact you about confirmation, deposit and balance arrangements.

Legal basis and retention

Processing for the registration request and related pre-contractual steps is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Processing required by applicable legal obligations is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Data are retained for the time needed to manage the event and for applicable statutory periods.

Recipients

We use Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, provided by Google, to receive and manage registration requests. Google states that data may be processed on servers outside the user's country. We do not claim a specific international-transfer safeguard without verification of the terms applicable to the account in use. Data are accessible to the organising team and necessary technical providers. We do not sell data or share them for third-party advertising.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to processing by writing to info@pepertango.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Cookies and analytics

The site does not load analytics tools or profiling cookies until they are configured with a compliant consent solution. Technical cookies that may be necessary for the service do not require consent.
