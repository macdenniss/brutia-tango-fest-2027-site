Data collected and purpose

Through the registration form we collect first name, last name, date of birth, email, telephone number, city and gender; dance role, registration type, any dance partner, selected package and classes or seminars; room-request details, including room type, dates, sharing preference and names provided; any notes and, if provided, a Facebook profile. We also record your response confirming that you have read the privacy notice. We use these data to manage registration for Brutia Tango Fest 2027, check availability and contact you about confirmation, deposit and balance arrangements. Fields marked as required in the form, including fields that become required depending on your choices, are necessary to submit your request; without them the form cannot be submitted. Other fields are optional unless the form makes them necessary based on your choices.
