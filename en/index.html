Location

Everything in one hotel.

Brutia Tango Fest takes place at the BV President Hotel, in Rende (CS): arrive, dance and rest in the same place, with no travel between event spaces, milongas and accommodation.

BV President Hotel · ★★★★

The weekend, without rushing.

The hotel is on Via Alessandro Volta 47/49 in Rende, conveniently located for central Rende, Cosenza and the University of Calabria.

  • At the hotel: event spaces, milongas and rooms
  • Rooms: select your preference in the form; availability and room type are confirmed by the organisers
  • Arrival: you will receive practical information after your registration is confirmed
